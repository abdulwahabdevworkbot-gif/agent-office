Reviews

Every time the Reviewer checks another agent's work: approved clean, approved with notes, or sent back with changes. Open a task to see each round, the changes asked, the sub-tasks opened for them and the chat.

Who reports to whom

From you down: your assistant and the Coordinator, then each department's Director, its group Managers and their people. Click anyone to open their page.

Calendar

Each task takes its estimate (agents work around the clock unless Settings say otherwise), after the tasks it depends on. When one of those runs late, everything after it moves, and the calendar shows why.

Room sizes

For each room: the most people it holds, and how full it gets before an exact copy opens for the next person. When fewer people need the room, the extra copy closes again.

Meetings and rooms

A meeting books its room. Small meetings take a huddle table; bigger ones the meeting room (10, 20 or 30 seats by head count). The meeting room is never booked twice at the same time.

Working hours

Calendars plan each task inside these hours. Agents run around the clock unless you set hours here.
