# Office page spec (card 0d)

The owner's requirements for the office page. Keep all of them when publishing or changing `office/office.html`.

## The page

- Entire page in English, left to right, left-aligned. Agents' names, descriptions, status and discussions are in English. Font: Dubai (shipped in `office/assets/fonts/`; never Thmanyah, its license forbids hosting the files). Title "Product Office", or "<product_en> Office" when `meta/office.product_en` is set.
- Art from W17ant/Claude-Office (MIT only; never its Dunder Mifflin / The Office fan art). Day and night rooms by local time.
- Four tabs: **Office**, **Roadmap**, **Chat** (with the count of open conversations) and **Task board**.
- Office tab: six rooms (Manager's office and Main office, then Meeting room and Huddle room, then Games & lounge and Idle room); hovering a room zooms it (stacked, no zoom, on phones).
  - Manager's office: the owner ("You") and `office-manager` as the assistant, seated at the desk; agents in state `waiting` come and stand here.
  - Meeting room: a conference table with chairs. Agents who are talking to each other (an agent-to-agent message in the last 15 minutes) and the members of every open conversation walk here and sit at the table; the owner takes the head seat. The room label names each open conversation.
  - Huddle room (the owner's rule): four small tables with four chairs each. The first open conversation takes the big meeting room; every next one takes a free small table, so people who arrive while colleagues are meeting sit at an empty table. The room label names each table's conversation ("Table 1: ..."). A group too big for its table stands beside it.
  - Main office: `working` and `blocked` agents sit on chairs at the desks.
  - Games & lounge: `done` and `idle` agents (arcade, ping-pong, foosball, bean bags, TV).
  - **Idle room** (the owner's rule): only rows of chairs. An agent with no activity for more than 3 days walks here, sits on a chair and does not move (no wandering, no coffee, no animation, a slightly faded sprite), with "Idle since <date>" over its head. Activity is the newest of: `last_active_at`, `updated_at`, `profile_updated_at`, the agent's last message, the last update of a task it owns, or a session it is a member of. Any new activity (a message, a task given to it, a meeting) brings it back to its normal room at once. `archived: true` sends a reusable agent here on purpose. The Coordinator, `office-manager` and `agent-designer` never go idle, and an agent `waiting` for the owner stays in the manager's office. The room label shows how many are inactive; the team list shows "Idle since <date>"; the assistant's summary names them. The idle room is the archive: agents worth reusing park here instead of being deleted.
  - **Rooms grow and shrink with the work** (the owner's rule, default): work rooms and meeting rooms are not a fixed number. When more than 10 agents sit at desks, another Main office opens ("Main office 2", "Main office 3", ...) beside the first; when more conversations are open than the meeting room and the huddle room's four tables can take, another Huddle room opens. When the load drops, the extra rooms close again once everyone has walked out. The first of each always stays (and is just drawn smaller when empty).
  - Each agent shows its sprite, an English name tag and a state bubble; clicking opens its card.
  - Agents move: when their state or meeting changes they walk (facing where they go) to the room's door, leave, and walk in to their new spot. A little animation keeps it alive: people in the lounge wander, people at desks fetch coffee now and then, and people in a meeting take turns talking (speech bubble, nod). Everything stays still when the viewer asks for reduced motion.
  - Under the rooms: "Your assistant says" summary with "Open the chat" and "Call a meeting", and the team list (name, description, state).
- **Roadmap tab** (the owner's rule: a room for the project's versions and phases, showing where the project is now from what the agents finished): every version of the system (First, Second, Third ...) is a card with its goal, its phases as steps (done, now, not started), a progress bar and "N of M done". A bar on top says **Where we are now**: the current version and its current phase (the first phase with open tasks), with how many tasks are done. The **phases** are, in order: **Planning** (stages To do, In progress, Done: planning the tasks and each agent's role), **Prototype**, **Staging** and **Production** (each with stages To do, In progress, Testing, Done). Clicking a version opens a summary kanban under it, general not detailed: rows are its phases (with Now / Done tags, how many are done, the agents on it as avatars that open their page, and "Open on the board", which opens the Task board filtered to that version and phase), columns are the stages, each cell a count with a few task names. Filters (the owner's rule: see everything, or only what you pick): Version, Phase and Stage checkboxes, "Show task names", "Open every version" and "Fold all". Task stages map to roadmap stages: todo → To do, doing and blocked → In progress, review → Testing (In progress in Planning), done → Done.
- **Chat tab** (the owner's rule: the chat gets its own tab): on the left, **Conversations**: "Team discussion" first, then every open session and meeting, newest activity first, each with its kind, title, members, last message and time; the one on screen is highlighted. Below it the **Archive**: closed meetings and conversations leave the open list and go here, folded, with a search over titles, resolutions, people and messages; opening one shows it read only. On the right, the discussion with the owner's composer (to, task, "this is a decision"); the message box grows as you type. On phones the list sits above the chat.
- **People in a conversation** (the owner's rule): an open session or meeting has a "People" button on the discussion bar listing every agent with a checkbox. Ticking adds that agent, unticking removes them, at once and without stopping the meeting; the Coordinator posts a note ("DevOps joined the meeting."). The owner and the Coordinator always stay.
- **Agent page** (the owner's rule): clicking an agent (in a room or the team list) opens its page (`#agent/<id>`): name, role, state and what it is doing now; **Look**: pick one of the ten characters and change the hair color and the shirt color (the page recolors the sprite with the masks in `assets/masks/`, in every room and avatar); **Profile**: instructions, personality, more about them, and hobbies you can add or remove; Save writes them with `relayed: false` so the Coordinator passes the changes to that agent's session. Under it, the agent's **Tasks** (Open / Done / All, each opens the task, plus "Show on the board") and **Conversations** (Open / Archive / All, each opens the chat).
- **Mixed Arabic and English titles** read in the right order everywhere (each title is isolated with its own direction).
- **Pop-ups close on an outside click** (the owner's rule): every window and pop-up (task, session, Call a meeting, People, pickers) closes when you click outside it, and with Escape.
- Task board tab: columns are the stages (To do, In progress, In review, Blocked, Done); rows (lanes) are the agents, e.g. Business Analyst, Web Prototype, Mock API, Backend, plus a **Conversations** lane on top. Filter by agent, and with the shared filters below. Each card also shows its phase. Each card shows its feature label, English title, id and test-case count. Picking a feature shows how many of its tasks are done, a progress bar, counts per stage, and who is on each part.
- Clicking a task opens: English title, id and owner, stage, feature, summary, description, acceptance criteria and test cases (steps, expected result), plus "Ask about this task".
- **Task sessions** (the owner's rule): when the owner doesn't understand a task, they open a session on it from the task. A session is private to that one question: its members are the owner, the Coordinator (the manager), the task's writer (`author`, by default `business-analyst`), the task's owner session, and any `related` sessions. Its messages stay out of the main discussion. It shows in the "Task sessions" list, as "Question open" on the board card, and its members sit in the meeting room. It closes when the problem is solved: the owner presses "Mark as solved", or the Coordinator closes it after the owner agrees, with a one-line resolution. A closed session is read-only and stays linked from its task.
- **Links everywhere** (the owner's rule): every task id, session and board view is a link with its own address (`#task/<ID>`, `#session/<id>`, `#board/<ID>`), so it can be opened or shared directly. Each discussion message links to its task, its session (when one exists) and its card on the board; task and session ids written inside a message become links too. The task window has "Show on board" and "Open session"; the session window has "Open the task" and "Show on board"; "Question open" on a board card opens the session. "Show on board" switches to the Task board, filters to the task's feature, scrolls to the card and highlights it.
- **Versions** (the owner's rule): every task belongs to one version (`v1`, `v2`, ...). Done tasks count as closed. Every task picker (the discussion filter, the composer's Task, the board's Find) lists only the open tasks of one version, with chips to switch version; closed tasks never show in that quick list, but typing searches every task, closed ones too, and picking one shows what was said about it. The board opens on the current version, has Version chips with open counts, folds the Done column away ("N done", Show / Hide), and Find jumps to any task, opening its version and the Done column when needed.
- **Discussion filter:** "Show" filters the discussion to one task (its task-session messages included), with links to the task and its board card and "Show all discussions". Picking a task there also sets the composer's Task.
- **Talk bubbles open the conversation:** clicking the speech bubble over someone in the meeting room opens that conversation in the Chat tab: the session or meeting (and the composer then posts into it), or the two agents' messages to each other.
- **Call a meeting** (the owner's rule): a "Call a meeting" button opens a list of every agent on the product, all ticked; the owner unticks who they don't need (the Coordinator always stays, it runs the meeting), writes the topic and starts it. A meeting is a session with `kind: "meeting"` (task optional): it shows in "Sessions and meetings", its members sit in the meeting room, and it opens in the discussion panel. Each invited agent answers from its own role; the owner ends it with "End meeting".
- **Quick replies** (the owner's rule: answers come fast, each from its role): when the owner writes in a meeting, a task session or to an agent, the page asks Claude (the `sample` capability) to answer at once as the invited agents, each from its own role and the office data, shown with a "Quick reply" tag and an "answering" indicator while they come. Quick replies never claim work that the data doesn't show and never decide money, deletion, production, security or scope; the agents' own sessions follow up with real work.
- **Closing to Done** (the owner's rule): every open session and meeting has a "Mark done" button (on the discussion bar; press twice to confirm). A closed one moves to the Archive. When the owner says in the chat to close it ("close it", "we're done", "خلاص قفلوه"), the Coordinator closes it with a one-line resolution and a short closing reply. Closing is the Coordinator's job. Once closed, its members leave the meeting room and go back to their desks for their tasks, or to the lounge when they have nothing, where they wander and play now and then.
- **Enter sends** in every composer; Shift+Enter makes a new line (typing in an input method is left alone).
- **Reply language** (the owner's rule): agents answer in English by default, even when the owner writes Arabic. When the owner asks for Arabic (or another language) in a conversation, the agents switch to it and keep it for that conversation until asked to switch back (stored as `sessions/<id>.lang`). Message text shows with `dir="auto"` so Arabic reads right to left.
- **Decisions become work:** when the owner decides something (in a meeting, a session or the discussion), the Coordinator restates it and proposes the tasks that carry it out, each with its owner agent (a "Proposed tasks" message). "Add to board" puts the ticked ones on the kanban as To do (`MT-<n>`, `relayed: false`). The Coordinator then writes them into `docs/tasks/`, starts the work in the owning sessions, and opens a task session whenever something needs the owner's answer, the same way as when the owner talks to Claude in a project chat.
- **Every conversation has a chat** (the owner's rule): whenever two or more agents talk, it is a session with its own messages, never just two agents shown together. Its `kind` says what it is: task session (no kind, on a task), `meeting`, `question` (a quick question), `consult` (a consultation) or `chat`. Every conversation is on the kanban in the Conversations lane: open ones under In progress, closed ones under Done, with its kind, title, members and message count; clicking opens it.
- **Filters** (the owner's rule, the same on the board and in the discussion's "Show" list): first the version (chips: each version with its open count, and All versions), then **Phase** checkboxes (Planning, Prototype, Staging, Production; none ticked means all), then **Stage** checkboxes (To do, In progress, In review, Blocked, Done (closed)); Done is unticked by default, so closed tasks and conversations stay hidden until ticked, and the list says how many are hidden. Then **Feature** as a tree (the owner's rule): each feature is a parent checkbox with its tasks under it as child checkboxes (open with the arrow). Ticking the feature takes it with all its tasks; opening it lets you tick single tasks instead. Unticking one child of a ticked feature keeps the others; the parent then shows a dash and "2 of 3". Ticking every child turns it back into the whole feature. Any number of features or tasks at once; none ticked means all. Conversations follow the task they belong to. Search narrows further. The board shows only the ticked stages as columns; when what is ticked belongs to one feature, its progress summary shows. The discussion's list holds tasks and conversations: picking a task filters the discussion to it, picking a conversation opens its chat below.
- **Arabic replies read cleanly** (the owner's rule): in an agent's message with Arabic, every English run (a word, a name, a task id) goes on its own line, with the Arabic before and after it on their own lines; plain numbers stay in the sentence, punctuation sticks to the line before it, and a one-word joiner (و, أو) keeps two English names on one line. Task ids are links.
- **Answers go straight to the point** (the owner's rule): quick replies never start with a greeting and never introduce the agent ("Hi! I'm the Backend"); the name on the message already says who it is.
- **Back to where you were** (the owner's rule): following a link from one tab to another (Chat to Task board, Task board to Chat, Office to Chat, Roadmap to Task board ...) shows a "Back to <tab>" button at the bottom of the screen; it returns to that tab exactly as you left it (the same conversation or filters, and the same scroll position). Several steps can be undone one by one. Clicking a tab yourself is not a step.
- **You join a talk** (the owner's rule): when two agents are talking and you write in their talk, you join it: the composer says "Join in", your message and their answers stay in that talk (`messages.pair`), and in the office you walk over and sit with them ("... & You"). Writing in a session you weren't in adds you to its members. You sit with the talk you wrote in last.
- **Rooms size by who is in them** (the owner's rule): an empty room is smaller, a room with people is normal, and a room with 4 or more people is a little bigger; rooms resize smoothly as people move.
- **Text stays inside its box:** long messages, links and task titles wrap inside the discussion panel and the dialogs, and the composer's drop-downs never widen the panel, at any window size.

## Data (the page's `db`)
- `agents/<session-id>` also keeps the agent page fields: `instructions_en`, `personality_en`, `notes_en`, `hobbies[]`, `look` (`sprite`, `hair`, `shirt` as #rrggbb), `profile_updated_at`, `relayed`.
- **Looks** (the owner's rule): `agent-designer` gives each new agent a random character from the ten and its own shirt color (`look.sprite`, `look.shirt`); the owner can ask for another character, colors or gender at any time. An agent with no saved look still gets a shirt color of its own, so two agents on the same character never look identical.
- `agents/<session-id>` may also carry `last_active_at` (ISO time of its last real work) and `archived` (true parks a reusable agent in the idle room).
- Agent names (`name_en`) are always English, without exception (the owner's strict rule).
- `agents/<session-id>`: `desk` (order), `name_en`, `role_en`, `doing_en`, `state` (working / waiting / blocked / done / idle), `updated_at`.
- `tasks/<ID>`: `order`, `session`, `state` (todo / doing / review / done / blocked), `feature`, `title_en`, `summary_en`, `description_en`, `acceptance_en[]`, `test_cases[]` (`id`, `title_en`, `steps_en[]`, `expected_en`), `updated_at`. The Arabic title and summary stay in `docs/tasks/` for talking with the owner.
- Agents talking to each other always do it in a session (below); there is no `talking_with` field.
- `tasks/<ID>` may also carry `author` (who wrote it; default `business-analyst`) and `related[]` (other sessions linked to it).
- `messages/<id>`: `at`, `from`, `to` (session id, `owner`, `all`, or `session`), `task`, `session` (task session id, for session messages), `kind` (question / answer / decision / note / owner), `text` (English for agents), `decided_by`, and for owner posts `author_id`, `relayed`.
- `tasks/<ID>` also carries `version` (e.g. `v1`); `meta/office.current_version` may name the version the board and pickers open on (otherwise the first version with open tasks).
- `messages/<id>` may carry `pair` (`<agent>|<agent>`, sorted) when the owner joins two agents' talk; quick replies there carry it too.
- `sessions/<id>`: `kind` (`meeting`, `question`, `consult` or `chat`; absent for a task session), `version` (when it has no task; otherwise the task's version), `task` (optional except for task sessions), `title_en`, `opened_by`, `opened_at`, `members[]`, `state` (open / closed), `closed_at`, `closed_by`, `resolution_en`, `relayed` (false until `office-manager` has told the Coordinator about a new or closed session).
- `meta/office`: `product_en`, `versions[]` (`id` like `v1`, `name_en` like "First version", `goal_en`), listed in order; a version used by a task but not listed still shows.
- `tasks/<ID>` also carries `phase` (`planning` / `prototype` / `staging` / `production`). Without it the page guesses from the owner session (coordinator, reviewer, context-keeper, business-analyst and office-manager → planning; mock-api and the prototype sessions → prototype; the rest → staging; nothing guesses production); the analyst should always set it.
