# Office page spec (card 0d)

The owner's requirements for the office page. Keep all of them when publishing or changing `office/office.html`.

## The page

- Entire page in English, left to right, left-aligned. Agents' names, descriptions, status and discussions are in English. Font: Dubai (shipped in `office/assets/fonts/`; never Thmanyah, its license forbids hosting the files). Title "Product Office", or "<product_en> Office" when `meta/office.product_en` is set.
- Art: rooms, characters and seated poses are the owner's own pictures, cut out of the owner's sheets with `tools/cut/` (never redrawn). Each carries the credit tag `@Abdulwahabdev`: small, readable only when zoomed, on every character and seated body; clear on every room. Only the status effects are from W17ant/Claude-Office (MIT).
- **Everyone stands** (the owner's rule, 2026-10-23): the rooms are the owner's drawings without chairs, every agent stands at its spot (a desk, a table, a game) and every character keeps the direction it was drawn in; nobody sits or turns. Walking still goes around the furniture. Near-identical characters (c22, c29, c30, c32, c33, c34) are not offered in the picker.
- **Room sizes** (the owner's rule, Settings tab): for the Open office, the Games & lounge and the Idle room, the owner sets the most people a room holds and how full it gets (50 to 100%) before an exact copy opens for the next person; extra copies close once fewer people need them. Saved in `meta/office.rooms` as `{main|game|idle: {max, at}}`; defaults are every spot in the picture, at 100% for the Open office and 80% for the others.
- **Open office** (the owner's picture, 2026-10-23): the work room is a large open-plan office (L desk, two desks, shelves); rooms show two per row.
- **State colors** (council round 6): one color per state everywhere: waiting amber (`--warn`), blocked red (`--bad`), late orange (`--late`, also "moved by a late task" and the LATE tag), done green, working blue, idle grey; open sessions use a plain chip. Reviews use the same tokens. Every select and text field shares one style that follows the theme; links read as text, and monospace is kept for task ids and the clock. Needs-you cards keep their own height.
- **Phones**: the page has a viewport tag and never scrolls sideways; the tabs and the header counts scroll in their own row (fading at the edge), the board's agent column narrows to 96 px, and the header's subline is hidden under 600 px.
- **Walk the floor** (council round 5): a button in the Needs you header, "Walk the floor (N)", goes through what needs the owner one item per step ("2 of 5"): the agent's character, the item, what it needs, the agent's last three messages, its answer buttons and a reply box, with Back and Next. The last page, **All clear**, says how many were answered and lists the owner's day: today's meetings with their rooms, what is due today, what runs late and when open work will be done.
- **Hover card**: pointing at a character shows a small card: name and role, state, why it is blocked and what it needs, what it does now (until when) and what is next from its calendar, and how many of its tasks run late. Clicking still opens the agent page.
- **Since you left**: in the Needs you header line, small counts of what changed since this viewer's last visit (new messages, tasks done, new tasks, newly blocked agents, tasks running late, work sent back by review, new and closed meetings); each count opens where it points (done and late open their task lists), and × clears it. The last visit is kept in the viewer's browser only.
- Tabs: **Office** (with **Needs you** on top: who waits for the owner, who is blocked, tasks to approve and questions, each with its answer button), **Roadmap**, **Chat**, **Task board**, **Calendar**, **Reviews**, **Team** and **Settings**. The header counts open where they point.
- Office tab: six rooms (Manager's office and Main office, then Meeting room and Huddle room, then Games & lounge and Idle room); hovering a room zooms it (stacked, no zoom, on phones).
  - Manager's office: the owner ("You") and `office-manager` as the assistant, standing at the desk; agents in state `waiting` come and stand here.
  - Meeting room: a conference table with chairs. Agents who are talking to each other (an agent-to-agent message in the last 15 minutes) and the members of every open conversation walk here and stand at the table; the owner takes the head spot. The room comes in 10, 20 and 30 places and the page picks the size by head count. The room label names each open conversation.
  - Huddle room (the owner's rule): four small tables with four chairs each. The first open conversation takes the big meeting room; every next one takes a free small table, so people who arrive while colleagues are meeting stand at an empty table. The room label names each table's conversation ("Table 1: ..."). A group too big for its table goes to the meeting room.
  - Main office: `working` and `blocked` agents stand at the desks.
  - Games & lounge: `done` and `idle` agents (arcade, ping-pong, foosball, bean bags, TV).
  - **Idle room** (the owner's rule): only rows of chairs. An agent with no activity for more than 3 days walks here, stands at its spot and does not move (no wandering, no coffee, no animation, a slightly faded sprite), (the room label counts them and gives the longest idle date; the hover card gives each one's date, so labels don't pile up). Activity is the newest of: `last_active_at`, `updated_at`, `profile_updated_at`, the agent's last message, the last update of a task it owns, or a session it is a member of. Any new activity (a message, a task given to it, a meeting) brings it back to its normal room at once. `archived: true` sends a reusable agent here on purpose. The Coordinator, `office-manager` and `agent-designer` never go idle, and an agent `waiting` for the owner stays in the manager's office. The room label shows how many are inactive; the team list shows "Idle since <date>"; the assistant's summary names them. The idle room is the archive: agents worth reusing park here instead of being deleted.
  - **Rooms grow and shrink with the work** (the owner's rule, default): work rooms and meeting rooms are not a fixed number. When the desks are full, another Main office opens ("Main office 2", "Main office 3", ...) beside the first; when more conversations are open than the meeting room and the huddle room's four tables can take, another Huddle room opens. When the load drops, the extra rooms close again once everyone has walked out. The first of each always stays (and is just drawn smaller when empty).
  - Each agent shows its sprite, an English name tag and a state bubble; clicking opens its card.
  - Agents move: when their state or meeting changes they walk (facing where they go) to the room's door, leave, and walk in to their new spot. A little animation keeps it alive: people in the lounge wander, people at desks fetch coffee now and then, and people in a meeting take turns talking (speech bubble, nod). Everything stays still when the viewer asks for reduced motion.
  - Under the rooms: "Your assistant says" summary with "Open the chat" and "Call a meeting", and the team list (name, description, state), in a box of its own height with a Find field (name or role), so the page stays short.
- **Roadmap tab** (the owner's rule: a room for the project's versions and phases, showing where the project is now from what the agents finished): every version of the system (First, Second, Third ...) is a card with its goal, its phases as steps (done, now, not started), a progress bar and "N of M done". A bar on top says **Where we are now**: the current version and its current phase (the first phase with open tasks), with how many tasks are done. The **phases** are, in order: **Planning** (stages To do, In progress, Done: planning the tasks and each agent's role), **Prototype**, **Staging** and **Production** (each with stages To do, In progress, Testing, Done). Clicking a version opens a summary kanban under it, general not detailed: rows are its phases (with Now / Done tags, how many are done, the agents on it as avatars that open their page, and "Open on the board", which opens the Task board filtered to that version and phase), columns are the stages, each cell a count with a few task names. Filters (the owner's rule: see everything, or only what you pick): Version, Phase and Stage checkboxes, "Show task names", "Open every version" and "Fold all". Task stages map to roadmap stages: todo → To do, doing and blocked → In progress, review → Testing (In progress in Planning), done → Done.
- **The room beside the chat** (the owner's rule): in the Chat tab, the open conversation's room sits next to its chat (a third column; under the conversation list on narrower screens, stacked on phones): the meeting table with that conversation's members standing at it, a label with its title, and where it really is in the office ("In the meeting room" or "Huddle room 2 · Table 1"). Clicking a member opens their agent page.
- **Click outside to go back** (the owner's rule): when a link brought you to the chat (from the Task board, the Office or the Roadmap), a click anywhere outside the conversation list, the chat and its room returns you to the page you came from, exactly as you left it, the same as "Back to <tab>".
- **Departments** (the owner's rule): when the project grows, agents can be grouped in departments (e.g. Marketing). Each department is its own tab in the Office ("Headquarters" first, then one tab per department with its code and head count; the last tab you picked is remembered). A department tab shows its name and code, its people in one horizontal row (the Director first, then each group with its manager first, then seniors, juniors and interns; each card shows the name, position, years of experience and state, and opens the agent page), and the department's own work rooms below (another room opens for every 10 people at its desks). Meetings, the lounge and the idle room stay in Headquarters. Agents without a department live in Headquarters.
- **Department code before the name** (the owner's rule): every agent in a department shows its department code before its name everywhere (`MKT · Content Writer`): name tags, the team list (as a colored badge), the board, the chat and the agent page.
- **Positions** (the owner's rule), lowest to highest: Intern, then the job title (Developer, Analyst, Designer, Marketer...), Senior <job>, Consultant, Senior Consultant, Manager, Director. A big department has a Director (10+ years) over the whole department; each group, by its size, has a Manager under the Director; each group mixes seniors, juniors and interns. The position and years show on the agent page and the staff row.
- **Chat tab** (the owner's rule: the chat gets its own tab): on the left, **Conversations**: "Team discussion" first, then every open session and meeting, newest activity first, each with its kind, title, members, last message and time; the one on screen is highlighted. Below it the **Archive**: closed meetings and conversations leave the open list and go here, folded, with a search over titles, resolutions, people and messages; opening one shows it read only. On the right, the discussion with the owner's composer (to, task, "this is a decision"); the message box grows as you type. On phones the list sits above the chat.
- **People in a conversation** (the owner's rule): an open session or meeting has a "People" button on the discussion bar listing every agent with a checkbox. Ticking adds that agent, unticking removes them, at once and without stopping the meeting; the Coordinator posts a note ("DevOps joined the meeting."). The owner and the Coordinator always stay.
- **Agent page** (the owner's rule): clicking an agent (in a room or the team list) opens its page (`#agent/<id>`): name, role, state and what it is doing now; **Look**: pick one of the characters (`assets/chars/characters.json`) and change the hair color and the shirt color (the page recolors the sprite with the masks in `assets/masks/`, in every room and avatar); **Profile**: instructions, personality, more about them, and hobbies you can add or remove; Save writes them with `relayed: false` so the Coordinator passes the changes to that agent's session. Under it, the agent's **Tasks** (Open / Done / All, each opens the task, plus "Show on the board") and **Conversations** (Open / Archive / All, each opens the chat).
- **Mixed Arabic and English titles** read in the right order everywhere (each title is isolated with its own direction).
- **Pop-ups close on an outside click** (the owner's rule): every window and pop-up (task, session, Call a meeting, People, pickers) closes when you click outside it, and with Escape.
- Task board tab: columns are the stages (To do, In progress, In review, Blocked, Done); rows (lanes) are the agents, e.g. Business Analyst, Web Prototype, Mock API, Backend, plus a **Conversations** lane on top. Filter by agent, and with the shared filters below. Each card also shows its phase. Each card shows its feature label, English title, id and test-case count. Picking a feature shows how many of its tasks are done, a progress bar, counts per stage, and who is on each part.
- Clicking a task opens: English title, id and owner, stage, feature, summary, description, acceptance criteria and test cases (steps, expected result), plus "Ask about this task".
- **Task sessions** (the owner's rule): when the owner doesn't understand a task, they open a session on it from the task. A session is private to that one question: its members are the owner, the Coordinator (the manager), the task's writer (`author`, by default `business-analyst`), the task's owner session, and any `related` sessions. Its messages stay out of the main discussion. It shows in the "Task sessions" list, as "Question open" on the board card, and its members stand in the meeting room. It closes when the problem is solved: the owner presses "Mark as solved", or the Coordinator closes it after the owner agrees, with a one-line resolution. A closed session is read-only and stays linked from its task.
- **Links everywhere** (the owner's rule): every task id, session and board view is a link with its own address (`#task/<ID>`, `#session/<id>`, `#board/<ID>`), so it can be opened or shared directly. Each discussion message links to its task, its session (when one exists) and its card on the board; task and session ids written inside a message become links too. The task window has "Show on board" and "Open session"; the session window has "Open the task" and "Show on board"; "Question open" on a board card opens the session. "Show on board" switches to the Task board, filters to the task's feature, scrolls to the card and highlights it.
- **Versions** (the owner's rule): every task belongs to one version (`v1`, `v2`, ...). Done tasks count as closed. Every task picker (the discussion filter, the composer's Task, the board's Find) lists only the open tasks of one version, with chips to switch version; closed tasks never show in that quick list, but typing searches every task, closed ones too, and picking one shows what was said about it. The board opens on the current version, has Version chips with open counts, folds the Done column away ("N done", Show / Hide), and Find jumps to any task, opening its version and the Done column when needed.
- **Discussion filter:** "Show" filters the discussion to one task (its task-session messages included), with links to the task and its board card and "Show all discussions". Picking a task there also sets the composer's Task.
- **Talk bubbles open the conversation:** clicking the speech bubble over someone in the meeting room opens that conversation in the Chat tab: the session or meeting (and the composer then posts into it), or the two agents' messages to each other.
- **Call a meeting** (the owner's rule, redesigned 2026-10-23 by the council while the owner slept): the topic comes first, with its kind (Discussion, Decision needed, Follow-up). The people it needs start ticked (the task's owner, writer, the owners of its dependencies and of the tasks after it, names and roles in the topic, the open conversation's members; up to 7, plus the Coordinator, who always runs it); "Everyone (N)" ticks all in one click, "Only the Coordinator" clears, with search and department chips. **When**: now, or later at a time (with "Find a free time" for everyone ticked, and a warning naming who is busy then); **How long**: 15/30/45/60 min. The footer shows the head count and the room it takes (up to 6 people a huddle table, then the 10, 20 or 30 seat meeting room) and stays in view. Start or Schedule writes a `kind: "meeting"` session with `purpose`, `duration_min`, `room` and `at`.
- **Quick replies** (the owner's rule: answers come fast, each from its role): when the owner writes in a meeting, a task session or to an agent, the page asks Claude (the `sample` capability) to answer at once as the invited agents, each from its own role and the office data, shown with a "Quick reply" tag and an "answering" indicator while they come. Quick replies never claim work that the data doesn't show and never decide money, deletion, production, security or scope; the agents' own sessions follow up with real work.
- **Closing to Done** (the owner's rule): every open session and meeting has a "Mark done" button (on the discussion bar; press twice to confirm). A closed one moves to the Archive. When the owner says in the chat to close it ("close it", "we're done", "خلاص قفلوه"), the Coordinator closes it with a one-line resolution and a short closing reply. Closing is the Coordinator's job. Once closed, its members leave the meeting room and go back to their desks for their tasks, or to the lounge when they have nothing, where they wander and play now and then.
- **Enter sends** in every composer; Shift+Enter makes a new line (typing in an input method is left alone).
- **Reply language** (the owner's rule): agents answer in English by default, even when the owner writes Arabic. When the owner asks for Arabic (or another language) in a conversation, the agents switch to it and keep it for that conversation until asked to switch back (stored as `sessions/<id>.lang`). Message text shows with `dir="auto"` so Arabic reads right to left.
- **Decisions become work:** when the owner decides something (in a meeting, a session or the discussion), the Coordinator restates it and proposes the tasks that carry it out, each with its owner agent (a "Proposed tasks" message). "Add to board" puts the ticked ones on the kanban as To do (`MT-<n>`, `relayed: false`). The Coordinator then writes them into `docs/tasks/`, starts the work in the owning sessions, and opens a task session whenever something needs the owner's answer, the same way as when the owner talks to Claude in a project chat.
- **Every conversation has a chat** (the owner's rule): whenever two or more agents talk, it is a session with its own messages, never just two agents shown together. Its `kind` says what it is: task session (no kind, on a task), `meeting`, `question` (a quick question), `consult` (a consultation) or `chat`. Every conversation is on the kanban in the Conversations lane: open ones under In progress, closed ones under Done, with its kind, title, members and message count; clicking opens it.
- **Filters** (the owner's rule, the same on the board and in the discussion's "Show" list): first the version (chips: each version with its open count, and All versions), then **Phase** checkboxes (Planning, Prototype, Staging, Production; none ticked means all), then **Stage** checkboxes (To do, In progress, In review, Blocked, Done (closed)); Done is unticked by default, so closed tasks and conversations stay hidden until ticked, and the list says how many are hidden. Then **Feature** as a tree (the owner's rule): each feature is a parent checkbox with its tasks under it as child checkboxes (open with the arrow). Ticking the feature takes it with all its tasks; opening it lets you tick single tasks instead. Unticking one child of a ticked feature keeps the others; the parent then shows a dash and "2 of 3". Ticking every child turns it back into the whole feature. Any number of features or tasks at once; none ticked means all. Conversations follow the task they belong to. Search narrows further. The board shows only the ticked stages as columns; when what is ticked belongs to one feature, its progress summary shows. The discussion's list holds tasks and conversations: picking a task filters the discussion to it, picking a conversation opens its chat below.
- **Arabic replies read cleanly** (the owner's rule): in an agent's message with Arabic, every English run (a word, a name, a task id) goes on its own line, with the Arabic before and after it on their own lines; plain numbers stay in the sentence, punctuation sticks to the line before it, and a one-word joiner (و, أو) keeps two English names on one line. Task ids are links.
- **Answers go straight to the point** (the owner's rule): quick replies never start with a greeting and never introduce the agent ("Hi! I'm the Backend"); the name on the message already says who it is.
- **Back to where you were** (the owner's rule): following a link from one tab to another (Chat to Task board, Task board to Chat, Office to Chat, Roadmap to Task board ...) shows a "Back to <tab>" button at the bottom of the screen; it returns to that tab exactly as you left it (the same conversation or filters, and the same scroll position). Several steps can be undone one by one. Clicking a tab yourself is not a step.
- **You join a talk** (the owner's rule): when two agents are talking and you write in their talk, you join it: the composer says "Join in", your message and their answers stay in that talk (`messages.pair`), and in the office you walk over and stand with them ("... & You"). Writing in a session you weren't in adds you to its members. You stand with the talk you wrote in last.
- **Rooms size by who is in them** (the owner's rule): an empty room is smaller, a room with people is normal, and a room with 4 or more people is a little bigger; rooms resize smoothly as people move.
- **Text stays inside its box:** long messages, links and task titles wrap inside the discussion panel and the dialogs, and the composer's drop-downs never widen the panel, at any window size.

## Calendar (the owner's rule, 2026-10-23)

- Every agent has its own calendar of its tasks and meetings: the **Calendar** tab (`#calendar`; "Everyone" lists each agent's now, next and when it is free, lates first; picking a person shows the next 7 days from today, with Everyone and Rooms as quick buttons; a task paused by a meeting shows in two parts around it), a week on the agent page, a **Time** row in the task dialog and a time line on each board card.
- Each task has `estimate_min` (default 60) and `depends_on[]` (task ids that must finish first). The plan is computed in the page, never stored: per agent, tasks run one after another (in progress first, then by order), each starting no sooner than the end of its dependencies; meetings and sessions (`at` or `opened_at`, `duration_min`, default 30) are busy time that tasks flow around. An open task ends no sooner than now, so a late dependency pushes everything after it.
- `due_at` (or `due`) marks a deadline: a task planned past it shows "Late by ...", and a task pushed by a late dependency shows "Moved: waits for X, which runs late". The Calendar tab's count is the number of late tasks.
- **Plan health** sits above the Everyone list: when all open work will be done, and counts of late tasks, tasks moved by a late task, dependency loops, tasks without an estimate and tasks without a deadline. A count lists its tasks with the reason; clicking one opens the task.
- **Plan baseline** (council round 5): the plan is locked once a day, on the owner's first visit after the plan is built (or with **Lock the plan now**), as `plans/<YYYY-MM-DD>` (`at`, `by`, `done_by`, `tasks: {id: {e, est}}`). Plan health then says how far the done-by date moved since the lock, and lists each task that slipped more than 15 minutes (old end → new end, and why: re-estimated, waits for a late task, or took longer) and each task added after the lock.
- Agents work around the clock by default; `meta/office.workday` (`start`, `end` hours, `days` 0-6) sets working hours.
- A meeting set for later is a session with `state: "scheduled"`, `at` and `duration_min`: it shows in its members' calendars, under "Coming up" at the top of the Chat conversations, in the meeting room's label ("Free · next ..."), and in Needs you an hour before (Start now). At its time it opens by itself and its people walk into its room. Meetings take the big meeting room before task sessions and talks, the biggest first; a meeting for a huddle table takes a table. When a meeting passes its planned length, Needs you shows "Time is up" with Wrap up.
- **Meetings and rooms** (the owner's rule, 2026-10-23): a meeting books its room. The meeting room's and the huddle room's labels always show their next booking, busy or free, and clicking it opens the Rooms calendar. The meeting dialog has a Room choice (By head count, Meeting room, Huddle table), an agenda (one point per line, prefilled from the task and what it waits for) and Repeat (Once, Every day, Every week; the next one is booked when one opens, `series` links them). The meeting room is never booked twice at the same time: the dialog warns of a clash and of members already busy, and "Find a free time" picks the next slot when everyone and the room are free. The Calendar's **Rooms** view shows the week's bookings per day with "+ Book a meeting". In the chat, a meeting set for later has **Start now**, **Move** (warns if the meeting room is booked then) and **Cancel meeting** (asks twice); an open meeting shows its agenda as a checklist (`agenda_done`) and **Wrap up** asks for the outcome, saves it as a decision (so it shows under Your decisions and in the task's minutes) and closes the meeting. Meetings link to their task from the Meeting button on late and blocked cards and in the task dialog, or from a task id typed in the topic.
- **Standing meetings** (council round 5): Settings turns on a **Daily stand-up** (time, 10/15/30 min) and a **Weekly review** (day, time, 30/45/60 min), saved in `meta/office.rituals` (`standup: {on, time, min}`, `review: {on, day, time, min}`), off by default. Each books its next time by itself as session `ritual-<kind>-<YYYY-MM-DD>-<HHMM>` (one id per date and time, so two open tabs can't double-book) with `ritual`, `agenda`, `tasks[]` and `members` taken from the plan: late tasks, blocked agents (with why) and agents waiting for a decision; the review also opens with the week's done count and the done-by date. When it opens (by itself or with Start now), the agenda and tasks are refreshed; the people booked stay as they are. Its minutes reach every task in `tasks[]`. Changing the time, day or on/off rebooks the next one of that kind only; changing the length just updates it; a meeting moved by hand (`moved: true`) is left alone.
- **Snooze** (council round 8): each Needs-you card has **Later** (1 hour, 3 hours, tomorrow 09:00). The card leaves the box and the count until then, saved in `meta/office.snooze` (`{card key|time|title: ISO time}`, so a new question or a new quiet agent shows as a new card); "N snoozed" lists them, each with **Wake**. Only the owner's page offers Later. Nothing is answered for the owner.
- **What a meeting costs** (council round 8): the meeting dialog plans the week once with the meeting and once without, and says which tasks it pushes and by how much, marking any that become late.
- **Sticky tabs** (council round 8): the tab bar stays at the top while scrolling, with an amber "N need you" pill that jumps to Needs you.
- **Waiting time** (council round 8): Waiting and Blocked cards say how long the agent has waited (`agents.waiting_since`, else `updated_at`) and come first, longest wait first.
- **Quiet agents** (council round 8): an agent marked working with no sign of life for max(2 h, 1.5 × its task's estimate) gets a Quiet card with **Ask for status** (a `kind: "question"` message); three or more fold into one card with "Ask them all" (5 seconds to Undo).
- **Tab badges** (council round 8): a number on a tab means "look here": Office = things for you, Chat = conversations where it's the owner's turn, Task board = blocked tasks, Calendar = late tasks.
- **Room door board** (council round 8): a room label's "Next …" (or "Book a time") link opens that room's next 24 hours: bookings, including open task sessions using the room (click to open), and free gaps of 30+ minutes, each with **Book**, which opens the meeting dialog with the time and room filled in. The week view is one link away.
- **Find anything** (council round 8): **Find** in the tab bar, or Ctrl+K, searches people, tasks, meetings and conversations, and messages and decisions; arrows move, Enter opens.
- **Time left** (council round 8): an agent may write `tasks.remaining_min` with `tasks.progress_at`. While the task is in progress and the report is under 6 hours old (and newer than its start), the plan ends it at progress_at + remaining instead of start + estimate.
- **Why this time?** (council round 8): the task dialog's Time row, and the hover on a calendar block, explain the plan in one line: started at / reported time left / waits for a dependency / after the previous task in the agent's queue, the estimate, meetings in between, and any late task that moved it.
- **Quiet filters** (council round 8): Roadmap filters fold into one summary line with **Change**; Reviews hides its filters while there are no reviews.
- **Before you go** (council round 10): a button in the Needs-you header (highlighted after 17:00) opens the end-of-day check: what's still open, **Snooze** the open cards to 09:00, except blocked ones, with 5 seconds to Undo (answers nothing), what's due before then, the next meeting, and a one-line note to the Coordinator (`kind: "note"`).
- **Halos** (council round 10): in the office, whoever is blocked glows red, waiting amber, and late orange, the same colors as Needs you.
- **Outbox** (council round 10): owner messages still `relayed: false` after 10 minutes show as "N not picked up yet" in the Needs-you header, with a list. It appears only once agents have marked any doc `relayed: true`, so agents should set `relayed: true` (and `relayed_at`) when they handle an owner doc.
- **Time and order in the task dialog** (council round 10): **Change the estimate, deadline or order** edits `estimate_min`, `due_at` and `depends_on`, with a live preview of what moves ("Moves 3 tasks later; BE-2 now late"). A dependency that would loop can't be picked. Saving tells the agent with a `kind: "update"` message and sets `plan_changed_by: "owner"`.
- **Follow a task** (council round 10): **☆ Follow** in the task dialog (owner only) keeps up to 8 tasks in a "Following" strip above the office, each with its state, end time or lateness. Saved in `meta/office.follow` (task ids).
- **Cards point at people** (council round 10): pointing at a Needs-you card lights that agent in the office and dims the rest; clicking its picture scrolls to them.
- **What sets the date** (council round 10): the task dialog shows the chain that sets a task's start (the dependency or the previous task in the agent's queue), late links in orange; Plan health shows the chain behind the finish date.

- **Estimates that learn** (council round 10): Settings switch `meta/office.learn_estimates` (owner only). When on, the plan multiplies each agent's estimates by the median of actual / estimate over its last 10 done tasks, counting working minutes only (needs `started_at` and `done_at`, 3 tasks or more, kept between 0.5 and 3). Why this time names the factor.

- **Phones: compact Needs you** (council round 12): under 520 px each card is one row (who, what kind, how long) and opens on tap; the header keeps Walk the floor, with the rest behind **More**.
- **A done moment** (council round 12): when a task turns done, its agent glows gold for a moment and a notice says "BE-1 Orders service · done by Backend" (grouped when several finish together). Still glow only with reduced motion.
- **Ready but not started** (council round 12): Plan health counts tasks whose dependencies are all done 30+ minutes ago, not started, with an agent who has nothing in progress, with **Nudge** (5 seconds to Undo). An optional `tasks.handoff_en` on the finished task says what was handed over.
- **Without you** (council round 12): "N changes without you" in the Needs-you header lists what changed since the last visit without the owner's tap: meetings opened on schedule, missed, or booked by themselves (with **Cancel it**, owner only, 5 seconds to Undo), conversations closed by others, finished tasks folded into one row, deadlines moved by agents, the automatic plan lock.
- **Reply templates** (council round 12): chips under each reply box: "Yes, go ahead", "No, wait", "Explain in one line" fill the box; "I'll answer when I'm back" sends with 5 seconds to Undo and snoozes the card for 3 hours.
- **Night in the office** (council round 12): from 18:00 to 06:00 a blue tint lies over the rooms (an overlay; the art is unchanged) with the people, labels and credit on top.
- **Who waits on whom** (council round 12): the hover card says "Waiting on: <agent> (BE-2 needs BE-1)" and "Waiting on them", from `depends_on` across agents plus an optional `agents.blocked_on` (a task or agent id); Plan health names the biggest bottleneck for the next 24 hours ("4 tasks wait on Backend"), counting only work that is late, blocked or not started 30+ minutes past its planned start.
- **Decided by others** (council round 12): Chat's Decisions box has **Yours** and **By others**: decisions by others (not restatements of the owner's, not from meetings the owner sat in) and closures with an outcome of conversations the owner wasn't in, each with **Ask to hold** (one message after 5 seconds, with Undo).
- **Last 7 days** (council round 12): Calendar's Everyone view opens with a folded card: tasks done, decisions and meeting minutes in the past week, the slowest tasks against their estimate, and today's slips against the locked plan.
- **Greeting line** (council round 12): under the office name, one line by the time of day: how many things need the owner, how many tasks run late, and when open work finishes.
- **Answered cards fold away** (council round 12): a Needs-you card the owner answered shows its "Sent" line for 2.5 seconds, then folds; Undo still works from the notice. The answer covers that version of the card: a new question from the same agent comes back as a new card.
- **You set this** (council round 12): times set in the time editor are kept in `tasks.owner_set` (`{estimate_min, due_at, depends_on}`). If an agent later changes one of them, Needs you shows "Changed what you set" with **Keep mine** (puts it back after 5 seconds, with Undo) or **Accept**; the task shows "Set by you: …". Values are compared by meaning (a number, a time, a set of tasks), so a different spelling of the same value is not a change.
- **Last night** (council round 14): after 3+ hours away, a strip above the cards shows what happened since the last visit as dots on a time line (green done, red blocked, blue meeting, amber missed, gold decided by others, grey plan lock), merged per 30 minutes. Hovering or focusing a dot points at the agents; clicking opens it. **Play the night** steps through up to 12 events with a caption and a glow on each agent, and ends on what needs the owner now (a list with reduced motion). The × on "since" clears it. The last visit counts only after 2 minutes on the page, so a quick app switch keeps the night for later.
- **Answering frees N** (council round 14): Waiting and Blocked cards say "Answering unblocks 4 tasks (BE-2, QA-1 +2)": the open tasks whose every open need lies in that agent's task or the chain after it (a task still held by other work is not counted). These cards sort first, and the greeting names the first one: "Start with DevOps: answering unblocks 4 tasks."
- **Walk the floor, one thumb** (council round 14): under 520 px Walk the floor fills the screen, and a bar at the bottom holds the card's answers, **Back**, **Later** (snooze 3 hours, with "Snoozed · Undo" for 5 seconds) and **Next**. Swipe left on the card for Later, right for Next.
- **Members gather** (council round 14): 5 minutes before a scheduled meeting the owner is in, its members walk to its room (blocked or waiting members stay put), and the room reads "Gathering · Daily stand-up · 09:00 · 5 coming · 1 busy". People in a live conversation stay in it, and a gathering never takes the meeting room from a live meeting (it uses a huddle table). It lasts until the meeting opens or its planned time is over. Next 3 hours adds "gathering now". Nothing is written; the meeting still opens on time as before.
- **While you were away** (council round 14): after 6+ hours away, one card at the top of Needs you says what was done, what went wrong (late, newly blocked) and who waits for the owner (and for how long), with **Start my round** (Walk the floor) and **Got it**. It replaces the "since" chips.
- **Warm start** (council round 14): the page keeps a copy of the office in this browser (agents, tasks, recent sessions, the last 100 messages; under 1.5 MB, saved each minute and when hidden, only while live). The copy is tied to the signed-in person and keeps no messages; it is deleted when the data can't be opened. On the next open within a day, by the same person, it shows that copy at once with "As of <time> · updating…" until live data arrives; automatic writes wait for live data, every Needs-you button and Walk the floor are disabled ("Updating…"), "Connected" shows only once agents, tasks and sessions have all arrived, and changes since the copy don't count as "just done".
- **Light through the day** (council round 16): over every room a light follows the local clock: a warm low sun from 06:00 to 09:00, clear by day, amber from 16:00 to 18:00, and the night blue from 18:00 to 06:00. The scene badge reads MORNING, DAY, EVENING or NIGHT. It is an overlay; the art is unchanged and people stay on top. Rooms have a soft frame and inner shade, the space between them is a dim corridor floor, and each person has a small shadow at their feet.
- **Calmer bubbles** (council round 16): in each room only one working agent shows the typing bubble at a time, taking turns every few seconds (fixed with reduced motion); the others have a soft screen glow at their spot. In meetings and huddles only the speaker's bubble is clear.
- **Live-link watchdog** (council round 8): a listener error, or the tab waking after 5+ minutes hidden, shows "Reconnecting…" and subscribes again. Automatic writes wait while data is stale or the tab is hidden.
- **Missed meetings** (council round 7): a scheduled meeting whose whole time plus 15 minutes passed while no owner page was open is closed with `missed: true`, never opened late. A repeating meeting then books its next future time once, as `<series>-<YYYY-MM-DD>`.
- **Who writes by itself** (council round 7): opening due meetings, booking standing meetings and locking the day's plan run only from the owner's page. `meta/office.owner_id` names the owner's user id; without it any writer may.
- **Minutes on the task**: the task dialog lists its meetings and sessions under "Meetings and minutes": coming ones (time, people) and closed ones with the outcome, how many agenda points were covered and the decisions sent in them.
- **Settings** has **Meetings and rooms** (`meta/office.meetings`: `huddle_max`, the most people for a huddle table, default 4 (four places per table); `default_min`, the default length, 30) and **Working hours** (`meta/office.workday`; Around the clock is the default).

## Data (the page's `db`)
- `agents/<session-id>` also keeps the agent page fields: `instructions_en`, `personality_en`, `notes_en`, `hobbies[]`, `look` (`sprite`, `hair`, `shirt` as #rrggbb), `profile_updated_at`, `relayed`.
- **Looks** (the owner's rule): `agent-designer` gives each new agent a random character from the ten and its own shirt color (`look.sprite`, `look.shirt`); the owner can ask for another character, colors or gender at any time. An agent with no saved look still gets a shirt color of its own, so two agents on the same character never look identical.
- `agents/<session-id>` may also carry `department` (a department id), `group` (its group's name), `position` (`intern` / `junior` / `senior` / `consultant` / `senior-consultant` / `manager` / `director`), `track_en` (the job word for juniors and seniors: Developer, Analyst, Marketer...), `years` (years of experience) and `reports_to` (the agent id of its manager).
- `meta/office.departments[]`: `id`, `code` (2 to 4 letters, e.g. `MKT`), `name_en`, `goal_en`, `color` (#rrggbb, optional), `director` (agent id), `groups[]` (`id`, `name_en`, `manager`).
- `agents/<session-id>` may also carry `last_active_at` (ISO time of its last real work) and `archived` (true parks a reusable agent in the idle room).
- Agent names (`name_en`) are always English, without exception (the owner's strict rule).
- `agents/<session-id>`: `desk` (order), `name_en`, `role_en`, `doing_en`, `state` (working / waiting / blocked / done / idle), `updated_at`, and when blocked `blocked_en` (why) and `needs_en` (what it needs from the owner). A task may carry its own `blocked_en`. Needs you and the board card show "Why: ..." and "Needs from you: ..."; without them the agent's last message stands in. A need written "Approve X" gets one-tap **Approve** / **Not now** buttons on its Needs-you card, and "Decide: A, or B" gets one button for each choice; a tap shows "Sending to <agent>: <text>" with **Undo** for 5 seconds, then sends the owner's decision to that agent; a failed send says so on the card.
- `tasks/<ID>`: `order`, `session`, `state` (todo / doing / review / done / blocked), `feature`, `title_en`, `summary_en`, `description_en`, `acceptance_en[]`, `test_cases[]` (`id`, `title_en`, `steps_en[]`, `expected_en`), `updated_at`. The Arabic title and summary stay in `docs/tasks/` for talking with the owner.
- Agents talking to each other always do it in a session (below); there is no `talking_with` field.
- `tasks/<ID>` may also carry `author` (who wrote it; default `business-analyst`) and `related[]` (other sessions linked to it).
- `messages/<id>`: `at`, `from`, `to` (session id, `owner`, `all`, or `session`), `task`, `session` (task session id, for session messages), `kind` (question / answer / decision / note / owner), `text` (English for agents), `decided_by`, and for owner posts `author_id`, `relayed`.
- `tasks/<ID>` also carries `version` (e.g. `v1`); `meta/office.current_version` may name the version the board and pickers open on (otherwise the first version with open tasks).
- `messages/<id>` may carry `pair` (`<agent>|<agent>`, sorted) when the owner joins two agents' talk; quick replies there carry it too.
- `sessions/<id>`: `kind` (`meeting`, `question`, `consult` or `chat`; absent for a task session), `version` (when it has no task; otherwise the task's version), `task` (optional except for task sessions), `title_en`, `opened_by`, `opened_at`, `members[]`, `state` (open / closed), `closed_at`, `closed_by`, `resolution_en`, `relayed` (false until `office-manager` has told the Coordinator about a new or closed session).
- `meta/office`: `product_en`, `versions[]` (`id` like `v1`, `name_en` like "First version", `goal_en`), listed in order; a version used by a task but not listed still shows.
- `tasks/<ID>` also carries `phase` (`planning` / `prototype` / `staging` / `production`). Without it the page guesses from the owner session (coordinator, reviewer, context-keeper, business-analyst and office-manager → planning; mock-api and the prototype sessions → prototype; the rest → staging; nothing guesses production); the analyst should always set it.

## Department status bar

The owner's rule: under the department tabs (Headquarters too), the bar shows:

- The Gregorian date and the time, updated every 30 seconds.
- Counts of that department's tasks:
  - Due today (`due` is today);
  - Done today;
  - Late (open, `due` before today);
  - In progress, In review, Blocked and To do.

Clicking a count lists its tasks. Each row has:

- the owner agent: avatar, name linking to `#agent/<id>`, and position;
- the task: its id linking to the board (`#board/<id>`), title, state, due date and summary.

The rooms point at those agents with a bouncing marker. Clicking the count again or pressing Escape closes the list.

New task fields: `due` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, or `due_at` ISO) and `done_at` (ISO; `updated_at` is the fallback). A task belongs to the department of its `session` agent.

## Team tab

The owner's rule: the Team tab (`#team`) shows the project's hierarchy as a top-down chart.

- **Top:** the owner, with the assistant (`office-manager`) beside them.
- **Under the owner:** the Coordinator.
- **Under the Coordinator:** the headquarters agents and each department's Director.
- **Under a Director:** the group Managers and anyone without a group.
- **Under a Manager:** the group's people.

An agent's `reports_to` overrides this. Cycles fall back to the owner. Each card shows the avatar, the department code, the name, the position or role and the state, and opens the agent's page.

## Reviews tab

The owner's rule: the Reviews tab (`#reviews`, with a count of tasks whose latest review asked for changes) lists every task the Reviewer checked.

- **Each row:** the latest result, the task (linked to the board), its owner agent, the number of rounds, how many times it was sent back, and sub-tasks done out of total.
- **Opening a row:** shows each round:
  - its reviewer and time;
  - the notes;
  - the changes asked;
  - the sub-tasks opened for them, with their state and owner;
  - a link to the chat where it was discussed.
- **Filters:** agent, reviewer, times sent back (1+, 2+, 3+), latest result (approved with no notes / approved with notes / changes requested) and "only with open sub-tasks".

Data: `reviews/<id>` holds `task`, `round`, `reviewer`, `outcome` (`approved` / `approved-notes` / `changes`), `at`, `notes_en`, `changes_en[]`, `subtasks[]` (task ids) and `session` (chat id). A task may also carry `parent` (the task it was split from), which counts as a sub-task too.


## Agent contract

What agents may write, and the form the page expects. A value in the wrong form is ignored or guessed, and listed under Settings → Data check.

General rules: every time is ISO 8601 with a time zone (`2026-10-24T09:00:00Z`); every id must exist; after handling a doc the owner wrote (`relayed: false`), set `relayed: true` and `relayed_at`.

| Collection | Field | Expected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| agents | state | working, waiting, blocked, done or idle |
| agents | desk | a number |
| agents | updated_at, waiting_since, last_active_at | ISO time with a time zone; set `waiting_since` when you start waiting on the owner |
| agents | blocked_en, needs_en | short text: why blocked, and what is needed from the owner ("Approve X" or "Decide: A, or B" give one-tap answers) |
| tasks | title_en | required |
| tasks | state | todo, doing, review, done or blocked |
| tasks | session | an agent id |
| tasks | estimate_min, remaining_min | minutes (estimate above 0, remaining 0 or more) |
| tasks | due_at, started_at, done_at, progress_at | ISO time with a time zone |
| tasks | depends_on | a list of task ids that exist |
| sessions | state | scheduled, open or closed |
| sessions | at, opened_at | ISO time with a time zone |
| sessions | members | agent ids, or owner |
| sessions | task | a task id that exists |
| sessions | room | meet or huddle |
| sessions | duration_min | minutes above 0 |
| messages | at | required, ISO time with a time zone |
